Section A: Mechanics & Waves
1. Which of the following is the SI unit of gravitational potential energy? [Marks: 1]
  • A) Watt
  • B) Joule
  • C) Newton
  • D) Pascal
Answer: B) Joule
2. An object accelerates uniformly from rest at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds. What is its final velocity? [Marks: 2]
  • A) 5 m/s
  • B) 10 m/s
  • C) 15 m/s
  • D) 20 m/s
Answer: B) 10 m/s
Section B: Modern Physics
3. Who proposed the photoelectric equation based on Planck's quantum theory? [Marks: 1]
  • A) Albert Einstein
  • B) Niels Bohr
  • C) Isaac Newton
  • D) Marie Curie
Answer: A) Albert Einstein
